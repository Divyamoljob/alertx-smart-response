Essential Women Safety Legal Rights & Protocols

Zero FIR Right
You can register an FIR at ANY police station irrespective of jurisdiction if in distress. Police cannot refuse registration.
Right to Virtual FIR / Email
Women can lodge complaints via email or registered post to the Senior Superintendent if unable to visit in person.
Right to Privacy in Statement
Statements must be recorded by a female police officer in a private, comfortable location with presence of trusted guardian.
